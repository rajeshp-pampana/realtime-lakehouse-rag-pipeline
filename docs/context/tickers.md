# Portfolio ticker reference

Sector and one-line context for each ticker the pipeline tracks. Useful for
grounding a briefing in what kind of company/catalysts actually move the
stock, beyond the raw price series.

- **MSFT** - Microsoft. Mega-cap software/cloud (Azure); moves on cloud
  growth, AI capex, and enterprise software demand.
- **CRWD** - CrowdStrike. Cybersecurity (endpoint protection); sensitive to
  enterprise IT security spend and its own incident history.
- **AVGO** - Broadcom. Semiconductors and infrastructure software; tied to
  AI/datacenter chip demand and networking hardware cycles.
- **GLE.PA** - Societe Generale (Euronext Paris). French universal bank;
  moves on European rates, credit conditions, and bank capital rules.
- **NVDA** - Nvidia. GPU/AI accelerator leader; the most direct proxy for AI
  infrastructure spending sentiment.
- **AMZN** - Amazon. E-commerce plus AWS cloud; watched for both consumer
  spending strength and cloud margins.
- **AXON** - Axon Enterprise. Public-safety hardware/software (TASER, body
  cameras); grows with government/law-enforcement contracts.
- **PANW** - Palo Alto Networks. Cybersecurity platform; a bellwether for
  enterprise security budgets.
- **INTC** - Intel. Legacy chipmaker in a turnaround; sensitive to foundry
  execution and PC/server chip demand.
- **NOW** - ServiceNow. Enterprise workflow/ITSM software; a SaaS
  growth-and-margins story.
- **IREN** - Iris Energy. Bitcoin mining / AI datacenter infrastructure;
  volatile, tracks crypto prices and power-cost economics.
- **GOOG** - Alphabet. Search, cloud, and AI; watched for ad revenue and
  Gemini/AI competitive positioning.
- **MU** - Micron Technology. Memory chips (DRAM/NAND); highly cyclical,
  tracks memory pricing cycles.
- **SOFI** - SoFi Technologies. Consumer fintech/neobank; sensitive to
  interest rates and consumer credit quality.
- **PLTR** - Palantir. Data analytics/AI software for government and
  enterprise; trades on large contract wins and AI narrative.
- **RDW** - Redwire. Space infrastructure/manufacturing; small-cap, moves on
  contract awards and the broader space-sector narrative.
- **DRAM** - a memory-sector proxy ticker in this portfolio, grouped with MU
  for cross-checking the memory-chip cycle story.
